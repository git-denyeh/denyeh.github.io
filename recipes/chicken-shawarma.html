Chicken Shawarma

Levantine street-style chicken

Spiced and cooked hot so the edges get a little char while the inside stays moist. The marinade is non-negotiable — yogurt + acid + spices is what turns ordinary chicken into proper shawarma.

Method

  1. Make the marinadeWhisk yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, olive oil, and all spices until smooth. Taste — it should be boldly seasoned and pleasantly sharp.
  2. MarinateCoat the chicken thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, ideally overnight (up to 24 h). The yogurt and acid tenderize while the spices penetrate.
  3. Cook(choose one)
    • Stovetop / grill pan (closest to street style): Heat a heavy skillet or grill pan over medium-high until very hot. Add a little oil. Cook chicken in a single layer 4–6 minutes per side until deeply browned and internal temperature reaches 74 °C / 165 °F. Rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.
    • Oven: Spread on a sheet pan and roast at 220 °C / 425 °F for 18–22 minutes, flipping once. Broil the last 2–3 minutes for extra char.
    • Outdoor grill: High heat, 4–5 minutes per side.
  4. Slice and serveCut into thin strips. Pile into warm pita with the usual toppings and a generous amount of Toum. The contrast of hot spiced chicken, cool vegetables, and sharp garlic sauce is the whole point.

Tips

  • Thighs over breast. More intramuscular fat = stays juicy under high heat and takes the marinade better.
  • Don’t skip the yogurt. It carries the spices and keeps the meat tender.
  • High heat is essential. You want browning and slight charring on the edges — that’s the Maillard contribution that makes it taste like real shawarma.
  • Make-ahead: Cooked chicken keeps 3–4 days in the fridge and freezes well. Reheat quickly in a hot pan.

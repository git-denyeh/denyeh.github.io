Rice noodles with egg and greens

Wide rice noodles, egg and greens in a very hot pan. The sauce gets made right there. No bottle required.

Makes
3–4 servings
Prep
20 min
Cook
10 min
Total
30 min
Difficulty
Medium
Dairy-freePeanut-freeNo seafoodWeeknight
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Soak noodles until pliable but not mushy. Drain.
  2. Heat wok or large skillet very hot with oil. Add garlic, stir 10 seconds.
  3. Add chicken, cook until just done. Push to side.
  4. Crack in eggs, scramble quickly.
  5. Add greens, stir-fry 1–2 minutes until bright.
  6. Add noodles, fish sauce, oyster sauce, dark soy, and pepper. Toss everything together 1–2 minutes until noodles are coated and slightly charred in spots.
  7. Serve immediately with lime and chili.

Tips

  • The sauce is made in the pan — no bottled stir-fry sauce.
  • High heat keeps it from getting soggy. Don't overcrowd the pan.

Based on traditional Thai street/home style, scratch sauce

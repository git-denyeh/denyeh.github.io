Shish Tawook

Lebanese grilled chicken skewers

Chunks of chicken in a bright yogurt, garlic and lemon marinade, grilled hard for char and still juicy inside. The sandwich version gets garlic sauce and pickles.

Method

  1. Make the marinadeWhisk yogurt, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, tomato paste, and all spices until completely smooth. Taste it — it should be boldly garlicky, tangy, and well-seasoned.
  2. MarinateAdd the chicken chunks and toss until every piece is thoroughly coated. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight (up to 24 hours). The yogurt + acid tenderizes while the garlic and spices work their way in.
  3. Skewer & grillThread the chicken onto metal or soaked wooden skewers, leaving a little space between pieces so heat can circulate. Why this works: Direct high heat creates the Maillard crust and slight char that defines good tawook while the yogurt keeps the interior moist.
    • Outdoor grill or grill pan: Medium-high heat. Grill 4–6 minutes per side, turning until nicely charred on the edges and the internal temperature hits 74 °C / 165 °F.
    • Broiler: Arrange on a foil-lined sheet and broil 5–7 minutes per side, watching closely for char.
  4. Rest & serveRest the skewers 3–4 minutes. For sandwiches: warm the pita, spread a generous layer of Toum, pile on the chicken chunks, add pickles, and roll tightly. For a plate: serve the skewers over rice with extra Toum and pickles on the side.

Tips

  • Don’t under-marinate. Four hours is the minimum for real flavor penetration; overnight is noticeably better.
  • Char is not optional. Pale chicken is just cooked chicken. You want some deep browning and light blackening on the edges — that’s the restaurant flavor.
  • Toum is non-negotiable for the authentic sandwich experience. A thick layer acts as both flavor and moisture barrier.
  • Make-ahead: Marinated raw chicken keeps 24 hours. Cooked tawook keeps 3–4 days in the fridge and reheats beautifully in a hot skillet (don’t microwave if you can avoid it).

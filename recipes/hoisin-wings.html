Hoisin Baked Chicken Wings

Wings tossed in hoisin and honey and baked until sticky. The oven does the work. You take the credit.

Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Whisk the hoisin, soy sauce, honey and sesame oil together in a big bowl.
  3. Toss the wings in it until every piece is coated. Lay them out in a single layer with a little room between them.
  4. Bake 40 to 45 minutes, flipping halfway, until crisp at the edges and 165°F inside.
  5. Scatter the chives and sesame seeds on top. Have napkins ready.

Tips

  • Works as an appetizer or as dinner if nobody's watching.

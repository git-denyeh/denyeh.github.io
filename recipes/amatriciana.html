Amatriciana

Guanciale, tomato, pecorino, a little chili. No onion, no garlic, and Rome would like you to keep it that way.

Makes
4 servings
Prep
15 min
Cook
30 min
Total
45 min

Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Render the guanciale in a dry skillet over medium heat 5–8 minutes until the fat melts and the meat is golden but still tender. Remove most of it, leaving a little in the pan.
  2. Add the chili and the wine. Scrape up the browned bits and let the wine evaporate completely.
  3. Add the tomatoes, a pinch of salt, and simmer 20–25 minutes, stirring now and then.
  4. Return the guanciale to the pan. Cook the pasta al dente, reserving a cup of pasta water.
  5. Toss the pasta with the sauce, adding pasta water as needed. Take off the heat and stir in the grated Pecorino until creamy.
  6. Serve with extra Pecorino and a grind of pepper.

Tips

  • Traditional recipe has no onion and no garlic — just guanciale, tomato, pecorino, chili, and wine.
  • Guanciale is essential; pancetta is a decent substitute but changes the flavor.
  • The official Amatriciana Tradizionale was registered in Italy's Gazzetta Ufficiale in 2020.

Based on La Cucina Italiana / official Amatriciana Tradizionale

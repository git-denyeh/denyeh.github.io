Cacio e Pepe

Cheese, pepper, pasta water. Three ingredients and exactly one way to ruin it: too much heat.

Makes
2–4 servings
Prep
10 min
Cook
15 min
Total
25 min
Difficulty
Medium
VegetarianWeeknightHigh-flavor
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Toast the peppercorns in a dry pan until fragrant, then crush them coarsely.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil with very little salt. Cook the pasta 1–2 minutes less than package directions. Reserve the starchy water.
  3. In a warm (not scorching) pan or bowl, combine the grated Pecorino with a few ladles of hot pasta water. Stir or whisk vigorously into a smooth, ricotta-like cream. Add the pepper.
  4. Add the drained pasta to the cheese cream. Toss constantly off the heat, adding more pasta water a splash at a time until the sauce coats every strand.
  5. Serve immediately with extra Pecorino and pepper.

Tips

  • Only three ingredients: pasta, Pecorino Romano, and pepper. The fourth is the pasta water that makes the emulsion.
  • Heat is the enemy — too hot and the cheese seizes into lumps. Work off the heat.
  • An aluminum pan helps; avoid nonstick for the cheese step.
  • No butter, oil, or cream in the authentic Roman version.

Traditional Bolognese Sauce

The two-hour version

The classic, a little less fussy. Still two hours, but the stove does most of the work.

Makes
4-6
Prep
20 min
Cook
2 hr 40 min
Total
3 hr
Make-ahead
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Chop the onion, celery and carrot fine, by hand. A food processor turns them to mush.
  2. Render the pancetta in the olive oil in a big deep skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat.
  3. Add the vegetables, drop the heat to medium-low, and stir until they're soft. No color. Patience here.
  4. Back up to medium. Add the beef, break it up, and cook about 10 minutes until it sizzles and browns.
  5. Pour in the wine and cook until it's completely gone.
  6. Stir in the tomato paste and passata, then 1 cup of boiling stock. Bring it to a simmer, turn it to low, and leave it uncovered for about 2 hours. Add hot stock whenever it looks dry.
  7. About an hour in, stir in the milk.
  8. Season with salt and pepper. It's done when it's thick, glossy and dark red.

Tips

  • Freezes well. Make a double or triple batch. Future you will be grateful.
  • Based on the Bologna recipe registered by the Accademia Italiana della Cucina.

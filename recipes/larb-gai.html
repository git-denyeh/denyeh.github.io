Larb Gai

Minced chicken salad

Warm minced chicken hit with lime, fish sauce, chili and a fistful of herbs. Fast, loud and light.

Makes
3–4 servings
Prep
15 min
Cook
10 min
Total
25 min
Difficulty
Easy
Gluten-freeDairy-freePeanut-freeNo seafoodWeeknightHigh-flavor
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. In a dry skillet or wok over medium-high heat, cook the ground chicken, breaking it up, until just cooked through and lightly browned. No oil needed if using thigh.
  2. Remove from heat. While still warm, stir in fish sauce, lime juice, and chili to taste.
  3. Fold in shallot, mint, and cilantro.
  4. Sprinkle with toasted rice powder if using — it adds crunch and traditional texture.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature with cucumber or lettuce.

Tips

  • Bright, sour, salty, and herbaceous — no sugar, no bottled sauce.
  • The toasted rice powder is the secret to authenticity; easy to make at home.
  • Naturally portion-friendly and very light.

Roasted Vegetable Soup

Roast a sheet pan of vegetables, blend them, call it soup. That's honestly all there is to it.

Makes
2-4
Total
25 min
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Spread out the peppers, sweet potato, onion, carrots, garlic head and all the tomatoes. Coat them with olive oil, herbes de Provence, and more salt and pepper than feels polite.
  3. Roast 15 to 20 minutes, until tender and a little charred. If the carrots or sweet potato still fight a fork, give them more time.
  4. Squeeze the soft garlic out of its skins into the blender. Add everything else from the pan.
  5. Blend, pouring in stock slowly until it's as thick or thin as you like. Add coconut milk or cream if you want it richer. Hot soup expands, so vent the lid and hold a towel over it.
  6. Warm it back up in a pot. Serve with a few croutons on top.

Tips

  • Roasting is the flavor. Don't pull the pan early just because the timer says so.

Adapted from The Fresh Market (recipe by Anna Rossi)

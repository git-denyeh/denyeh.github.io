One-Pot Mediterranean Chicken with Orzo & Spinach

Chicken, orzo and spinach in one pan, with za'atar and lemon doing the heavy lifting. One pan to wash.

Makes
4
Total
30 min
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Heat 2 tbsp of the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the chicken, 1 tbsp of the za'atar and the salt. Toss to coat.
  2. Brown the chicken on all sides, 5 to 6 minutes. It doesn't need to be cooked through yet. Move it to a plate.
  3. Drop the heat to medium. Add the last tablespoon of oil and the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the spinach and garlic and give it 3 to 4 minutes more.
  4. Add the broth, orzo, the rest of the za'atar and the chicken. Stir, bring it to a boil, then cover and simmer on medium-low for 12 minutes. Stir once halfway so the orzo doesn't glue itself to the pan.
  5. Take it off the heat and stir in the lemon juice and oregano. The chicken should be at 165°F by now.
  6. Bowl it up, top with feta, and eat it while it's hot.

Tips

  • Za'atar is a thyme, sumac and sesame blend. Most grocery stores carry it now.

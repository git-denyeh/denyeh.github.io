Parmesan Cream Pasta

Cream, parmesan, lemon, linguine. Not subtle, not trying to be.

Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Boil the linguine in well-salted water for 7 to 9 minutes, until al dente. Before you drain it, scoop out about 1 cup of the pasta water.
  2. Meanwhile, warm the cream with the bay leaves in another pot over medium heat. Let it simmer 10 to 15 minutes, stirring now and then, so it thickens a little.
  3. Take it off the heat and fish out the bay leaves. Whisk in the lemon juice, then the parmesan a handful at a time so it melts smooth instead of clumping.
  4. Pour the sauce over the pasta and season with salt and pepper. Loosen it with splashes of pasta water until it coats the noodles instead of sitting on them.
  5. Chives on top. Done.

Tips

  • Add the parmesan off the heat. Hot cream plus cheese all at once equals clumps.

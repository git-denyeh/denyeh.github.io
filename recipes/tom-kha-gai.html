Tom Kha Gai

Coconut chicken soup

Chicken in coconut broth with lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves. Sour, salty, fragrant, done in about half an hour.

Makes
4 servings
Prep
15 min
Cook

Method

  1. In a pot, bring stock to a gentle simmer. Add lemongrass, galangal/ginger, and lime leaves. Simmer 5 minutes to infuse.
  2. Add coconut milk and bring back to a low simmer (do not boil hard).
  3. Add chicken and mushrooms. Cook 5–7 minutes until chicken is just done.
  4. Remove from heat. Stir in fish sauce and lime juice to taste — it should be sour, salty, and fragrant.
  5. Discard lemongrass and large lime leaves if desired. Garnish with cilantro and extra chili.

Tips

  • Traditional versions use fresh coconut cream; canned is acceptable if unsweetened and BPA-free lined.
  • No sugar added — the lime and fish sauce balance naturally.
  • Good with jasmine rice.

How to Cook & Puree Beans

Technique

How to cook dried beans soft enough to disappear into sauces, soups and chili. Protein and fiber, no bean texture.

Method

  1. Pureeing removes the texture and most of the taste that bothers you.
  2. You still get the protein and fiber (about 15 g fiber per cooked cup).
  3. Works for Italian dishes, chili, or any recipe that needs a creamy base.
  4. Soakovernight in cold water — about 3 cups water per 1 cup beans. Drain and rinse.
  5. Simmerin fresh water with 1 bay leaf and 1 smashed garlic clove. About 1–1.5 hours until completely soft. No salt until the end (keeps skins tender).
  6. They’re ready when they mash easily against the side of the pot.
  7. Pureewith an immersion blender or in a blender until smooth. Add a little of the cooking liquid if needed.

Best beans for pureeing

  • Cannellini (white kidney) — classic Italian, creamy, blends silky. Great Northern works the same.
  • Pinto or kidney — good for bolder flavors like chili.
  • Prefer dried over canned for the cleanest result.

Tips

  • Start small: a tablespoon or two stirred into something you already like, such as chili or tomato sauce.
  • Blend them into minestrone, spread a white bean puree under roasted vegetables, or hide them in a smooth sauce.
  • If you still don't like them, don't force it.

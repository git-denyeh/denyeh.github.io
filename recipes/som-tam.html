Som Tam

Green papaya salad, peanut-free

Green papaya pounded with garlic, chili and lime. Sour, hot and crunchy, with sesame standing in for peanuts.

Makes
2–3 servings
Prep
20 min
Total
20 min
Difficulty
Easy
VeganGluten-freeDairy-freePeanut-freeNo seafoodWeeknightHigh-flavor
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. In a mortar and pestle (or bowl with the back of a spoon), pound garlic and chili until crushed.
  2. Add tomatoes and green beans if using; pound lightly to bruise.
  3. Add papaya, pound gently to soften slightly.
  4. Season with fish sauce, lime juice, and a touch of sugar. Toss well.
  5. Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds. Serve immediately.

Tips

  • Toasted sesame seeds stand in for the peanuts. Same crunch, no peanuts.
  • Bright, sour, spicy, and refreshing — perfect light side or starter.
  • No cooking, very low calorie.

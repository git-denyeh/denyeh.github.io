Cucumber-Tomato Salad

Israeli-style salata

The high-acid, zero-cooking cucumber-tomato salad that every good falafel shop does right: pure crunch and brightness.

Method

  1. Dice the cucumbers and tomatoes into a consistent small dice (about ¼-inch). Place in a colander, sprinkle with ½ tsp of the salt, and let drain 5–10 minutes. This pulls out excess water so the salad stays crisp instead of watery.
  2. While they drain, finely dice the onion and chop the herbs.
  3. Combine drained cucumber and tomato with onion, parsley, and mint in a bowl.
  4. Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, remaining salt, pepper, and any optional spices. Pour over the vegetables and toss thoroughly.
  5. Taste and adjust: more lemon for brightness, more salt if flat, more oil if it needs richness. Let sit 10–20 minutes at room temperature (or chill briefly) so the acid and salt draw everything together.

Tips

  • Texture is everything. Uniform small dice is non-negotiable; big chunks feel clumsy and release water unevenly.
  • Acid first. The lemon does the heavy lifting—this is a high-acid salad on purpose. Taste after 10 minutes; the flavors bloom.
  • Make-ahead: Best the day it’s made. If prepping earlier, keep the dressing separate and toss just before serving so the cucumbers stay snappy.

Variations

  • Add a handful of crumbled feta and a few Kalamata olives for a fuller Mediterranean version.
  • Swap mint for a little chopped dill and add a spoonful of Greek yogurt for a creamier, cooler take.

Pure Green Smoothie

A copycat

A copy of the twelve-dollar green smoothie for about three. Mostly fruit, so it tastes like fruit.

Makes
1 smoothie (16–20 oz)
Prep
5 min
Total
5 min
Weeknight
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Blend coconut water, spinach, and kale until smooth, about 30 seconds.
  2. Add mango, pineapple, and banana; blend until thick and smooth, about 30 seconds more.

Tips

  • Makes one smoothie, 16 to 20 oz. A copy of Pure Green's 'Pure Green' smoothie: about $12 in the store, about $3 at home.
  • Roughly 3 parts fruit to 1 part greens keeps it sweet, not earthy.
  • Too green? Cut the kale to ¼ cup or use a spotted banana. Too thick? More coconut water.
  • Add protein powder or nut butter if you want it to hold you until lunch.

Pomodoro Sauce

Sugo di pomodoro

The plain red sauce everything else gets measured against. Five ingredients, half an hour, no sugar.

Makes
About 1 quart (for 1 lb pasta)
Prep
10 min
Cook
30 min

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic (and shallot if using) and cook 30–60 seconds until fragrant — do not brown.
  2. Add the tomatoes with their juices. Season with salt. Break them up with a wooden spoon.
  3. Bring to a gentle boil, then lower the heat and simmer uncovered 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.
  4. For a smoother sauce, blend with an immersion blender. Stir in the torn basil off the heat.
  5. Toss with cooked pasta and a splash of pasta water. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil.

Tips

  • Classic version uses only tomatoes, olive oil, garlic, basil, and salt — no onion, no sugar.
  • San Marzano tomatoes give the sweetest, least acidic result.
  • Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge or freezes well.

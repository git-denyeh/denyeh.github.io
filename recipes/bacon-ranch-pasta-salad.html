Bacon Ranch Chicken Pasta Salad

A pasta salad that doesn't pretend to be health food. Bacon, chicken, a pile of vegetables and ranch.

Makes
4
Total
30 min
Scale

Ingredients

Quantities scaled. Times, temperatures and pan sizes don't scale; trust your eyes.

Method

  1. Boil the pasta per the package. When it's about two minutes from done, throw the zucchini, squash, bell pepper, broccoli and carrots into the same pot.
  2. Drain everything well. Toss it in a big bowl with the olive oil and ½ tsp each of salt and pepper. Set it aside.
  3. Fry the bacon in a heavy skillet over medium heat until crisp, about 7 minutes, flipping now and then. Move it to a paper towel. Leave the fat in the pan. That's flavor.
  4. Season the chicken with the rest of the salt and pepper. Cook it in the bacon fat about 3 minutes a side, until golden and 165°F inside. Let it rest a few minutes, then cut it bite-sized.
  5. Split the pasta and vegetables between four bowls. Top with the chicken, ranch (as much as you're willing to admit to), green onions and crumbled bacon.

Tips

  • Best at room temperature. Not bad cold the next day either.
